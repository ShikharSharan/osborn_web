FOR KIDNEY & CHRONIC-CARE FOLLOW-UP

The details that help
your doctor see the full picture.

Your doctor may recommend tests to understand kidney health, blood sugar, blood pressure or the effects of an ongoing condition. The right tests depend on your individual concern and medical history.

03

Helpful answers

Questions before
your visit.

For personal advice about your health, please contact the clinical team directly.

Do I need to fast before a test?+

Some tests may need fasting or specific preparation. Call the main centre before visiting, so the team can guide you according to the test requested.

Do I need to bring a prescription?+

Please bring a doctor’s prescription when you have one, along with previous reports and a current list of medicines. Call ahead if you are unsure.

When will my report be ready?+

Report timing depends on the specific test. Call the team for the latest information before your visit or while arranging the test.

Can I discuss my results with a doctor?+

Yes. If a clinical review is needed, call the clinic to arrange a consultation and bring the report with you.
