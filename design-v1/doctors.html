Doctor profile

DR. DEEPAK CHANDRA SRIVASTAVA

Specialist care for the conditions that need steady attention.

Dr. Srivastava works across internal medicine, kidney disease, hypertension, diabetes and preventive healthcare, with an emphasis on early diagnosis, lifestyle counselling and long-term wellness.

Training & experience

Medical training at JNMCH, Aligarh Muslim University; fellowship training in nephrology at Apollo Hospitals, New Delhi; clinical experience at AIIMS Gorakhpur and Apollo Hospital, New Delhi.

Clinical procedures

Experience in renal biopsy, central-line insertion, lumbar puncture and bone marrow biopsy. His official profile highlights 100+ successful internal jugular dialysis catheter placements.

Clinical focus

Kidney care, chronic disease management and informed prevention.

  • Kidney disease and CKD screening
  • Creatinine, kidney-function and urine-protein evaluation
  • Hypertension and diabetes management
  • Dialysis counselling and kidney-protection planning
