01

Kidney care

KIDNEY & DIALYSIS CARE

Focused care for kidney health, at every stage.

Kidney problems can develop slowly or appear suddenly. Our care focuses on timely evaluation, clear explanations and practical plans to protect kidney health and manage ongoing conditions.

We support care for

  • Chronic kidney disease (CKD)
  • Acute kidney injury
  • Diabetic kidney disease
  • Proteinuria and urine-protein evaluation
  • Creatinine and kidney-function evaluation
  • Electrolyte disorders and resistant hypertension
Dialysis support

Dialysis counselling, kidney-protection planning and emergency dialysis support for clinically appropriate cases.

Resources & FAQs

Helpful answers
before you visit.

For personal diagnosis or an emergency, please contact the clinical team directly.

How can I book a consultation?+

Call the clinic most convenient for you. The Bargadwa clinic can be reached at +91 97609 01297.

What should I bring for a kidney or medical consultation?+

Please bring previous prescriptions, test reports, discharge summaries and a current list of medicines whenever available.

Can I ask about dialysis support?+

Yes. Call ahead to discuss dialysis counselling or urgent kidney-related concerns with the clinic team, so you can be guided appropriately.

How do I check whether a diagnostic test is available?+

Call before visiting for the latest test availability, preparation requirements and report timelines.
