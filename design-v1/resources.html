01

Prepare for a kidney consultation

+

Bring previous test reports, prescriptions, discharge summaries and your current medicine list. Write down symptoms, changes and questions before you come, so the consultation can focus on what matters to you.

Meet Dr. Deepak
02

Keep your health records together

+

Keep reports, prescriptions and a current list of medicines in one folder or on your phone. It makes follow-up easier and helps the clinical team understand changes over time.

Learn about diagnostics
03

Make follow-up visits more useful

+

Ask about your treatment plan, when you should return and what information to track between visits. If anything is unclear, call the clinic rather than changing a medicine on your own.

04

Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers
before you visit.

If you are worried about a symptom, test result or medicine, call the clinical team for personal guidance.

How do I arrange a consultation?+

Call the clinic location most convenient for you. The Bargadwa main clinic can be reached at +91 97609 01297.

What should I bring for a kidney or medical consultation?+

Please bring previous prescriptions, test reports, discharge summaries and a current list of medicines whenever available.

How can I check whether a test is available?+

Call before visiting for the latest test availability, preparation requirements and report timelines.

Can I check medicine availability before travelling?+

Yes. Call the main centre with the medicine name and prescription details, so the team can guide you before you visit.

When should I call emergency services instead of waiting for a consultation?+

If you believe there is a medical emergency, call the main centre immediately. Emergency and critical-care support is available 24×7.
